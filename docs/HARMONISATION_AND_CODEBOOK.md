# Harmonisation and codebook summary

## Cohort
- Study years: 2015-2023.
- Primary cohort: admission records age >=20 years with principal ICD-10 S06.x diagnosis.
- Sensitivity cohort: age >=18 years where exact age was available.
- Unit of analysis: hospital admission, not a unique person.

## Core common variables
Country; calendar year; age/age band; sex; principal ICD-10 diagnosis; broad administrative TBI phenotype; in-hospital death; length of stay; hospital identifier where validated; region.

## Hospital identifiers
Validated longitudinal hospital identifiers were available only in Brazil and Mexico. Chile and Ecuador were retained for admission-level analyses and were not forced into hospital-volume inference.

## Outcome coding
Country-specific value labels and discharge-status coding were audited before harmonisation. Missing or non-informative values were retained as missing rather than coded as absence. Full source-specific decisions are documented in the source-validation and preflight scripts and version notes.

## Analytic freeze
The primary v2.8.4 cohort, outcomes, exposure, and models were frozen before manuscript preparation. v2.8.5.1 and v2.8.6.1 were methodological/interpretive sensitivities. v2.8.7 froze the publication roster and retired the non-informative spline display.
