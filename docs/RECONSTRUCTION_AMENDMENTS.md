## Logistic estimability amendment — 2026-11-03T21:35:40.625335+00:00

The originally specified Mexican S06.1–S06.7 hospital-intercept logistic model
failed the preserved weighted-information rank check. Independent inspection
showed a full initial within-hospital rank (20) and rank 19 at Newton iteration
26; the two other/unknown-sex records were both survivors, and their coefficient
diverged to approximately -24.5. This is quasi-complete separation, not grounds
to report the unconverged odds ratio as a finite MLE. The limiting numerical
coefficient was visible during diagnosis.

Retain the planned model as not estimable, with its sample size and same-sample
linear estimate. Preserve all checks and raw records. Add one separately
labelled post hoc diagnostic restricted to recorded male/female sex within
Mexican specified lesions. This excludes two records from the informative
logistic sample and changes its target population. It is not a replacement for
the planned inclusive model or a result-selected sensitivity. Report both.
The six planned logistic attempts remain; this creates a seventh attempt.
